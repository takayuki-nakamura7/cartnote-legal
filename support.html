Good to know.

A few everyday questions, answered.

How do I start the next shop?

Checked items stay in place. Tap a checked item to uncheck it, or choose Uncheck All from the list’s More menu. When every item is checked, an Uncheck All button also appears on the shopping screen.

Why does an item remain after I remove it?

Removing an item takes it off the current shopping list and clears its note. The saved item remains available in Search/Add so you can reuse it. Use the saved-item delete action to remove it permanently.

How do Suggested items work?

Suggested uses check-off counts and recent check-off dates stored on your device. Items already on the current list are excluded. No shopping data is sent to an online recommendation service.

Can I sync or share a list?

CartNote does not offer automatic sync or collaborative lists. The share button sends a text copy of the unchecked items still to buy, including notes, to a destination you choose. Changes made afterward do not update that copy.

To move your full collection, export a backup and import it on the other device.

How do I back up or restore my data?

Open Settings and choose Export Backup to save a JSON file. Keep the file somewhere you can access later.

Choose Import Backup to select a saved backup. After you confirm, the backup replaces the current lists, saved items, categories, language setting, and list settings. Import does not merge collections, and replacement cannot be undone in the app. Export your current data first if you want to keep it.

Why was a backup rejected?

Use a CartNote JSON backup supported by your installed app version. If the file was created by a newer version, update CartNote first.

Backup files must fit these limits:

  • File size: 10 MiB, or 10,485,760 bytes.
  • Up to 100 lists and 256 categories.
  • Up to 10,000 saved items across all lists.
  • Each list, category, or item name: up to 1,024 UTF-8 bytes.
  • Each item note: up to 16,384 UTF-8 bytes.

Byte limits are not character limits; Japanese characters commonly use multiple bytes. Damaged files and inconsistent records are also rejected. Exported backups must fit the same limits.

Can support recover deleted data?

There is no CartNote server copy for support to retrieve. Recovery depends on a backup you saved or a device backup managed outside the app. Deleting a list deletes its saved items. Deleting the app removes its local data; offloading the app retains its documents and data.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings and select English or 日本語. The interface changes immediately. Names and notes you entered are not translated. CartNote supports iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

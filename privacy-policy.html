CartNote · Privacy policy

Your lists. Your device.

CartNote works locally. Here’s how your data is handled in the app, in backups, and when you visit this website.

Your shopping data stays on your device

CartNote is an offline shopping-list app. It has no accounts, developer-operated app backend, automatic sync, analytics, or advertising. The app does not automatically send your lists or shopping data to the developer.

CartNote stores lists, saved items, notes, categories, display settings, and language preferences on your device. It also stores local check-off counts and related dates to generate Suggested items. These recommendations are calculated on your device.

Backups and sharing are your choice

You can export a JSON backup or share shopping-list text through destinations you choose. Backups include your lists, saved items, notes, categories, language setting, and list settings. Shared text includes the list name and the unchecked items still to buy, grouped by category, with their notes.

Exported JSON files are readable files without a CartNote password or additional encryption. A destination you select, such as cloud storage or a messaging app, may store or transmit that information under its own policies.

Depending on your device settings, operating-system backups may also contain CartNote data. Those backups are separate from the app’s export feature and are managed by Apple or your chosen backup destination.

Importing a backup replaces the current app data after you confirm. It does not merge collections, and the replacement cannot be undone in the app. Export your current data first if you want to keep it.

Keeping and deleting data

Checking an item or removing it from the current shopping list does not delete the saved item. Saved items remain available to add again. Removing an item from the current list clears its note.

You can delete saved items within the app. Deleting a list also deletes its saved items; shared categories remain. Deleting the app removes its local app data from the device. Offloading the app keeps its documents and data.

Deleting data in CartNote does not delete previously exported files, shared messages, or device backups. Manage those copies through the services or devices where you stored them.

Visiting this website

Visiting this website is separate from using the offline app. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect visitor IP addresses and other technical request information to deliver and operate the website. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for information about its processing.

Contacting support

If you email support, Takayuki Nakamura and the email services involved receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. That information is used to respond to and manage your request. Please include only information needed to explain the issue; you do not need to send your shopping lists or a full backup.

For privacy questions or requests concerning support correspondence, email support@takenocode.com. Copies held by external services remain subject to those services’ policies.
